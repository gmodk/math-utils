Quick reference guide

Purpose. Use this explorer to see a 3×3 correlation matrix as a Gram matrix of three unit vectors. Pairwise correlations become pairwise cosines/angles.

Controls / options

ItemMeaningHow to read it
ρ(X,Y), ρ(X,Z), ρ(Y,Z)Pairwise correlations among the three standardized variables.Values near +1 align vectors; near 0 make them close to orthogonal; near −1 point them in opposing directions.
yaw / pitchVirtual camera orientation.Changes only the view, not the mathematics.

Charts

ItemMeaningHow to read it

ItemMeaningHow to read it

Core mathematical identity

R is a Gram matrix: Rᵢⱼ = ⟨uᵢ,uⱼ⟩, with ‖uᵢ‖=1.
