Ellipsoid and PCs

Quick reference guide

Purpose. Use this explorer to interpret a covariance matrix geometrically. The ellipsoid axes are eigenvectors; axis lengths scale with square roots of eigenvalues.

Controls / options

ItemMeaningHow to read it
σ₁, σ₂, σ₃Marginal standard deviations.Increase one to stretch the covariance geometry in directions involving that variable.
ρ₁₂, ρ₁₃, ρ₂₃Pairwise correlations.They rotate and shear the covariance ellipsoid by changing off-diagonal covariance terms.
yaw / pitchCamera orientation.Changes display only.

Charts

ItemMeaningHow to read it
Wireframe ellipsoidA constant Mahalanobis-distance surface associated with Σ.Long directions correspond to high-variance linear combinations.
PC1, PC2, PC3 arrowsEigenvectors of Σ.PC1 is the direction of greatest variance; subsequent PCs are orthogonal directions with decreasing variance.

Metrics

ItemMeaningHow to read it
λ₁, λ₂, λ₃Eigenvalues of Σ in descending order.Each gives variance along the corresponding principal component.
PC1 shareλ₁/(λ₁+λ₂+λ₃).Large values mean one dominant common risk direction.
First 2 PCs share(λ₁+λ₂)/trace(Σ).Measures how faithfully the 3D system can be approximated by a plane.

Core mathematical identity

Σvᵢ = λᵢvᵢ; ellipsoid semiaxes are proportional to √λᵢ.
