Quick reference guide

Purpose. Use this explorer to study multicollinearity as near-linear dependence in the predictor space and as coefficient-estimation instability.

Metrics

ItemMeaningHow to read it
VIF1/(1−r²) for two standardized predictors.Large VIF means slope variance inflation from collinearity.

SD β̂₁, SD β̂₂Monte Carlo coefficient standard deviations.Direct empirical measure of estimation instability.

Core mathematical identity

With standardized predictors, VIF=1/(1−ρ²); as |ρ|→1, XᵀX becomes ill-conditioned and Var(β̂) grows.
