Quick reference guide

Purpose. Use this explorer to compare ordinary least squares with ridge and lasso as geometric constraints/shrinkage in coefficient space.

Controls / options

ItemMeaningHow to read it
λRegularization strength.λ=0 recovers OLS; larger λ shrinks coefficients more strongly.
corr(x₁,x₂)Predictor correlation.High correlation makes the OLS loss contours narrow/elongated and increases instability.
noise σNoise standard deviation.Higher noise makes the estimated optimum less stable.
sample sizeNumber of observations.Larger n makes the loss surface more concentrated.
resampleDraw a fresh synthetic dataset.Shows sampling variability at fixed hyperparameters.

Charts

ItemMeaningHow to read it
Coefficient-space geometryRSS contours around the OLS optimum plus OLS, ridge and lasso coefficient points.Ridge moves inward smoothly; lasso often lands on/near an axis, producing sparsity.
Coefficient shrinkage barsCompares β̂₁ and β̂₂ across OLS, ridge and lasso.Smaller magnitudes indicate stronger regularization.

Metrics

ItemMeaningHow to read it
OLS βUnpenalized least-squares estimate.Can have high variance under collinearity.
Ridge βMinimizer of RSS+λ||β||²₂.Continuous shrinkage; typically both coefficients remain nonzero.
Lasso βMinimizer of RSS+λ||β||₁.Can set coefficients exactly to zero.
RSS valuesResidual sum of squares for each estimate.Training fit usually worsens as regularization increases.
L1/L2 normsCoefficient size measures.Regularization trades fit against coefficient magnitude.

Core mathematical identity

Ridge: minβ ||y−Xβ||² + λ||β||²₂. Lasso: minβ ||y−Xβ||² + λ||β||₁.
