Quick reference guide

Purpose. Use this explorer to interpret Bayesian linear regression as the multiplication of a Gaussian prior by a Gaussian likelihood, producing a Gaussian posterior over coefficients and a posterior predictive distribution.

Core mathematical identity

Σpost=(XᵀX/σ²+I/τ²)⁻¹, μpost=Σpost Xᵀy/σ², and y*|x*,D is Gaussian with variance σ²+x*ᵀΣpost x*.

Coefficient ellipses have Mahalanobis radius 2; they are not 95% joint credible regions. The displayed interval is for β only, and OLS β provides the shrinkage comparison. Predictive limits use 1.96 SD. The source declares σ=0.80 on a step lattice starting at 0.15; browsers may normalize that initial slider value.
