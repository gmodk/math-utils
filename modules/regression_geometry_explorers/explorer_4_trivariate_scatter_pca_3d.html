Quick reference guide

Purpose. Use this explorer to connect raw joint observations, a correlation matrix, and PCA.

Controls / options

ItemMeaningHow to read it
ρ₁₂, ρ₁₃, ρ₂₃Target correlations for the simulated trivariate Gaussian.They control the orientation and elongation of the cloud.
samplesNumber of simulated observations.Larger samples make empirical geometry smoother.
yaw / pitchCamera orientation.Changes display only.
generate new cloudDraws a fresh random sample from the same selected distribution.Useful for distinguishing sampling noise from structural geometry.

Charts

ItemMeaningHow to read it
3D point cloudSimulated observations (X₁,X₂,X₃).Elongation indicates correlated directions.
PC1–PC3 arrowsEigenvectors of the selected correlation matrix.They summarize the dominant directions of the cloud.

Metrics

ItemMeaningHow to read it
PSD determinant conditionPositive-semidefinite feasibility condition.If negative, the correlation matrix is impossible.
λ₁, λ₂, λ₃Variance along PCs for standardized variables.They sum to 3.
First 2 PCs share(λ₁+λ₂)/3.High values mean the cloud is close to a plane.

Core mathematical identity

For standardized variables, trace(R)=3 and explained-variance ratio of PC k is λₖ/3.
