Regression, Correlation & Statistical Geometry — 11 Interactive Explorers

Each explorer is a self-contained HTML/JavaScript file and includes a quick-reference guide describing its controls, charts, metrics and core mathematical identity.

  1. Explorer 1 — 3D Correlation Vectors
    Correlation as inner-product geometry; Gram matrices; positive-semidefinite feasibility.
  2. Explorer 2 — 3D Covariance Ellipsoid & PCA
    Covariance ellipsoids, eigendecomposition, principal axes, explained variance.
  3. Explorer 3 — 3-Asset Portfolio Variance Surface
    Portfolio variance as the quadratic form wᵀΣw over the long-only simplex.
  4. Explorer 4 — 3D Trivariate Scatter & PCA
    Joint Gaussian samples, correlation geometry, PCA directions and dimension reduction.
  5. Explorer 5 — Simple Regression / Projection Geometry
    OLS as orthogonal projection onto span(xc), residual orthogonality and R²=cos²θ.
  6. Explorer 6 — Multiple Regression / Projection Subspace
    OLS projection onto span(x1c,x2c), normal equations, collinearity and fitted subspaces.
  7. Explorer 7 — Ridge / Lasso Regularization Geometry
    Penalized least squares, coefficient shrinkage and the geometric contrast between L2 and L1 penalties.
  8. Explorer 8 — Multicollinearity / VIF / Condition Geometry
    Near-linear dependence, VIF, condition number and sampling instability of coefficients.
  9. Explorer 9 — Bias–Variance / Polynomial Complexity
    Underfitting, overfitting and the pointwise bias² + variance + noise decomposition.
  10. Explorer 10 — Principal Component Regression
    PCA rotation, truncation, PCR coefficients, explained variance and predictive tradeoffs.
  11. Explorer 11 — Bayesian Linear Regression / Posterior Geometry
    Gaussian prior, Gaussian likelihood, posterior ellipses, shrinkage and posterior prediction.
